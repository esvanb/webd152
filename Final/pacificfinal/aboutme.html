Our Story

Pacific Trails Resort was founded to provide a unique coastal experience focusing on comfort, privacy, and nature. We want our guests to feel an authentic vacation experience by offering a peaceful luxurious retreat. We are committed to providing exceptional service and creating unforgettable memories for all our visitors and offer a lot of activities. So come on down to experience the best of the coast!

Why Choose Us?

    • Luxurious Ocean-View Yurts
    • Fine Dining with fresh local ingredients
    • Guided Nature Activities
    • Private and relaxing environment

Our Mission

We want to connect people with nature and each other in a peaceful, luxurious setting. Life can be simple, and we believe that the best experiences come from being surrounded by the beauty of the natural world and the warmth of human connection. There are many activities available for our guests to enjoy with dedicated guides waiting to greet you.
